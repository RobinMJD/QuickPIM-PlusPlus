PURPOSE: QuickPIM++ lists, activates, extends and deactivates Microsoft PIM access from one popup.

REQUIRED: Install the submitted Edge package. Use an authorized work/school account in a PIM-licensed Entra tenant with an ELIGIBLE Entra role. Personal accounts and permanent active assignments cannot test activation. Use the same Edge profile for extension and portal. No app registration or token entry is needed. No shared test account or demo roles are supplied.

TEST:
1. Pin QuickPIM++. Sign in at https://entra.microsoft.com/ and confirm eligibility in PIM > My roles.
2. Popup > Settings > Role Access > Open missing portal pages. Complete Microsoft sign-in/MFA/tenant selection if asked. Return and use Recheck now if needed.
3. Popup > Entra Roles > Refresh. Select an eligible role > Continue. Choose Activation time and fill required justification/ticket fields. Example reason: Microsoft Edge certification: verify temporary activation of the test account role. Generic "test" is rejected.
4. Click Activate 1 selected. This sends a REAL PIM request. The form returns to selection; View requests shows item, scope, duration, parameters and Microsoft status. Awaiting approval is expected if policy requires it.
5. Close/reopen during a request: the SAME operation remains in Requests. Submit another eligible item while it runs. Conflicting item/scope requests are blocked. Verify in Settings > Activity & Usage and Microsoft PIM.
6. Restart Edge: accepted/uncertain writes are not resent. Never-sent items wait for original-account access and may resume within 15 minutes.
7. Select an active PIM item > Continue > Disable 1 selected. An early five-minute-minimum rejection offers a cancellable Retry automatically at ... . Activity offers Extend for supported active requests.

PIM Groups/Azure Roles need separate eligibility; enable them in Settings > Popup & Appearance. Missing access shows empty/access states. Licensing, MFA and approval remain enforced. No developer backend.
