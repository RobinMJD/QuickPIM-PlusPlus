## Changelog

- Keep bundle results unread until every item outcome has been visible, including failures below the scroll area.
- Preserve open or focused result details when another request starts, and restore keyboard focus after dismissing the last result.
- Keep action buttons reachable when request details, queued disable retries, and access or sync notices fill the popup.
- Show recoverable request-status errors even when no cached request is available, clear stale error messages after recovery, and reset disclosure state when changing accounts.
- Keep accepted requests with unavailable tracking visible and protected against duplicate submission until tracking recovers.
- Preserve background execution, browser restart recovery, full Activity history, and the compact request summary.
