Changes since [v2.18.12](https://github.com/RobinMJD/QuickPIM-PlusPlus/releases/tag/v2.18.12).

## Changelog

Fixes scoped Entra role activation when the optional validation check reports that the role assignment already exists. The same role can be activated at the tenant and administrative-unit scopes in either order, including multiple administrative units.

QuickPIM++ now submits the unchanged requested scope once after an explicit validation-only duplicate response. Microsoft still enforces eligibility, MFA, approval, and genuine same-scope duplicate rules on the actual request. Actual failures and uncertain write outcomes are never converted into success or retried by this fallback.

Regression coverage includes localized service codes, both activation orders, independent scope selection and locks, scope-specific tracking, pending approvals, and rejected or ambiguous writes.
